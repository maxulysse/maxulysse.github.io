I made the [Sarek Interchange](https://maxulysse.github.io/sarek-interchange/), a metro map of the nf-core pipelines and resources that feed nf-core/sarek or use its results.

It is inspired by the [meta-omics metro map](https://vagkaratzas.github.io/meta-omics-sig/) that [Evangelos Karatzas](https://github.com/vagkaratzas) built for the nf-core meta-omics special interest group. Thanks to [Famke](https://github.com/famosab) and [Rike](https://github.com/FriederikeHanssen) for taking part in this plan.
